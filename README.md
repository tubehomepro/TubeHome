A modern, privacy-focused media player and streaming engine built with Android Jetpack Compose and ExoPlayer. Tube Home delivers a high-performance, clutter-free entertainment experience with zero telemetry, zero analytics, and no invasive device permissions.

---

## Highlights

- **Native Media Engine:** Powered by AndroidX ExoPlayer with adaptive HLS and DASH stream rendering.
- **Modern Design:** 100% Jetpack Compose and Material You design language with smooth edge-to-edge layouts.
- **Zero Privacy Footprint:** No accounts, no sign-ins, no location tracking, and zero advertising trackers.
- **TV & Wireless Casting:** Seamless screen streaming via local Wi-Fi multicast to smart displays.
- **Biometric Security:** Optional biometric app lock to keep your viewing preferences private on shared devices.

## Privacy & Permissions

Tube Home is engineered on the principle of data minimization:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | Required to stream remote media content. |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Detects offline/online connectivity status. |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Keeps the screen active during uninterrupted video playback. |
| `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE` & `MULTICAST` | Discovers local smart TVs and casting receivers. |
| `USE_BIOMETRIC` | Optional on-device fingerprint/face lock. |

**Zero Sensitive Permissions:** Tube Home requests **no** access to your storage, contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.
